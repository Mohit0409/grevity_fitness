Privacy notice

Clear handling of your information.

This notice describes the website data practices designed for Gravity Fitness. Last updated 28 August 2026.

REQUIRES_OPERATOR_LEGAL_REVIEW — This draft must be reviewed and approved by Gravity Fitness before a production launch.

Who is responsible

Gravity Fitness, Bungalow No. 41, 1st Floor, Above Canara Bank, CRPF Road, Neemuch Chawni, Neemuch, Madhya Pradesh 458441 is responsible for information submitted through this website. Privacy questions can be raised by calling or messaging +91 79995 26112.

Information collected

Public enquiries

When you send a visit, membership, coaching or general enquiry, the website collects your name, mobile number, optional email, enquiry type, selected membership plan where relevant, optional preferences and message. The server also creates a non-sequential reference, timestamps and workflow status.

Security and abuse prevention

The service uses short-lived security tokens, idempotency identifiers and one-way keyed hashes derived from network and contact information to prevent duplicate or abusive submissions. Raw network addresses are not stored in the public enquiry rate-limit table.

Member accounts

Member account features are currently unavailable publicly. If enabled after configuration, the service is designed to receive verified identity information from Firebase and store a first-party session plus member profile, membership, payment, receipt and coaching records needed to provide the account service.

How information is used

Enquiry information is used to respond to the request, confirm changing details, maintain an internal follow-up record, protect the service and document staff actions. It is not used by this website to make an automated booking or take an online payment.

Retention

Public enquiry records are assigned a 180-day retention date. Operational or legal needs may require Gravity Fitness to retain some records for a different period after operator review. Account, membership, receipt and security records follow their applicable operational and legal retention requirements.

Sharing and service providers

Authorised Gravity Fitness staff can access enquiries according to their role. The site is designed to use Firebase for authentication and Razorpay for online payments only after those services are configured and enabled; both are currently unavailable on the public site. A user-directed WhatsApp follow-up opens WhatsApp only when the user chooses that link.

Security

The service uses origin and request-token checks, first-party sessions, rate limits, role-based administrator access, multi-factor administrator authentication and audit records. No online service can promise absolute security.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit optional fields. To ask about access, correction or deletion of an enquiry, contact Gravity Fitness using the phone number above and provide the request reference if available. Identity may need to be confirmed before a request is actioned.

Updates

Gravity Fitness may update this notice as the service or legal requirements change. The date at the top identifies the current published draft.
