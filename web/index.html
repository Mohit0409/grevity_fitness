Good to know

Straight answers before you visit.

Does a visit request confirm my booking?

No. The website records your preference and gives you a reference. Gravity Fitness will follow up to confirm details.

What are the current membership prices?

1 Month is ₹1,200, 3 Months is ₹3,000, and 1 Year is ₹10,000.

Can I pay online?

Online checkout is currently unavailable. The enquiry form remains available and does not take payment.

Does Gravity issue a GST tax invoice?

Gravity Fitness currently operates in receipt-only mode and does not issue a GST tax invoice.

When is Gravity open?

Monday to Saturday, 6:00am to 10:00pm. Sunday is closed.
